How to Play

  • 👶
    Aputu crawls on his own. You can't steer him — but he's curious, and he always heads for the next hazard.
  • 👆
    Tap to stop traffic and shoo dogs. Drag carts and cones out of the way. Hold to calm a goat or switch off a wire.
  • ⭕
    Watch the ring. It shrinks and turns red as Aputu closes in — fix the hazard before it does.
  • 🦋
    He gets distracted by flowers, butterflies, balloons and cats. Those are free coins and breathing space.
  • 🚦
    Challenge events shake things up — traffic jams, storms, power cuts and parades.
  • 🎁
    Grab power-ups and pick a guardian whose perk suits your style.
  • 📖
    Fill Nanny's scrapbook by meeting every hazard, neighbourhood and memorable moment.
  • 💛
    If a hazard gets through, your guardian swoops in with a cuddle. Nothing scary — ever.
